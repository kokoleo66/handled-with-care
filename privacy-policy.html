Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026 · separate from our general Privacy Policy

This policy applies to consumers in Washington, Nevada, California, and other states with consumer health data laws. It covers "Consumer Health Data," information linked to you that identifies a past, present, or future physical or mental health status, including conditions, medications, biometric data, and health-related location data.

What we collect and why

Health conditions, medications, and care metrics you or your Huddle enter, strictly to display and coordinate that information within your family's account. We don't collect anything beyond what the app's features need, and we don't use it for any purpose beyond what's described here without asking you first. If you choose to attach a reference photo of a medication label or pill, that photo is stored securely and visible only to your Huddle until you remove it; this is separate from our photo-scanning feature, where photos are used once to read information and are never kept afterward.

Who it's shared with

We never sell, rent, or lease your health data. It's visible only to the family members you personally invite into your Huddle; you'll always see a clear prompt before an invite goes out. It's processed by four infrastructure providers strictly to run the app, under contracts that forbid them from using it for anything else, including training their own models on it: Netlify (application hosting), Supabase (database and authentication), Anthropic's Claude API (photo-scanning features), and OpenAI's Whisper API (voice note transcription).

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for your health data at any time by emailing nicole@genxcaregiver.care or visiting our privacy request page. We respond within 45 days, and you can appeal any decision we make.

Reading this policy

If you're using the app, you can also open this policy any time from Settings (tap your avatar, top right) without needing to leave the app. Agreeing to this policy at signup confirms you've had the chance to review it; it doesn't waive any of the rights described above.
